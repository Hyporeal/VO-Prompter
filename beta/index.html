My voice-over

Add your finished read (WAV, MP3 or M4A) and Listen mode can play it, sentence by sentence, with the guide following. Add more later (a pickup session, say): newer recordings replace older ones where they overlap. Finding the words runs on this device and takes a while for long recordings.

Test recordings

Saved while listening when Settings → Save test recordings is on. Save gives a .zip with the audio and the app's log, for testing. They hold your read of the script, so keep them private.

Scripts, edits, videos and recordings stay on this device. Nothing is uploaded.

Settings

Text
The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog.
The app hears each word about half a second after you say it. With overlap, the guide also covers the word it just heard and the next few, so the word you're saying stays inside it. Try More if you still read past it; None is a single exact spot.
The page waits until you've read this many lines or sentences before it moves. With sentences, the new sentence moves to your reading position; a sentence too long to fit still scrolls before you run out of room.
At a pause: moves only in a break between words. Slow and steady: drifts up gently instead of stepping. These two move every few lines or sentences (Scroll every), and always keep a couple of lines ahead in view. Continuous: the line you're reading stays at the reading position and the text moves up smoothly at your pace, like a teleprompter, so the next lines keep coming; best for long flowing reads such as audiobooks (Scroll every doesn't apply).
Screen
Interface size scales the bars, buttons and these panels; interface text size scales their text only. The script keeps its own text size (above). Both apply when you let go of the slider.
Following your voice
The built-in mic keeps Bluetooth earbuds out of phone-call mode. Applies to the on-device engine.
Also saves what the mic hears while listening, plus a log of what the app heard and where it put you, so the app can be tested on real reads. Files → Test recordings. On-device engine only; about 0.5 MB a minute.
Skipped words get a red wavy line. Possible misreads (dotted) are often the recognizer's mistake, not yours.
Listen mode
Read aloud / Pause is at the top. Tapping the sentence being read also pauses.
App

While on, the app notes what voices, video and the speech engine do, with times, so a problem can be traced. Turn it on, reproduce the problem, then Copy debug log and paste it into a message. No script words, file names or account details are logged.
Licenses

Diagnostics are app details for troubleshooting (version, settings, device, recent errors). They never include your script's words, file names or video.

Removes all scripts, videos, settings and the speech model from this device (shared with the main VO Prompter app, so it clears that too), then shows how to remove the icon.

Contents

Sections are headings, marker lines (like START SESSION 02) and bracketed directions that start with a capital (like [SLIDE: …] or [SYSTEM]). Highlights that touch count as one, across colours and sentences. Tap one to go there; the video follows.
